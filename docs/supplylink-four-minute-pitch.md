# SupplyLink by OceanPride — A Smart Delivery Recovery Solution

Four-minute pitch video. English narration, recorded local demo, longyi catalog, synthetic order and GPS.

## 0:00–0:25 — A paid order. A delivery problem.

Imagine a longyi retailer in Yangon preparing for a busy sales week. Sixty pieces have been ordered. The payment has been sent. Then the delivery runs into trouble. Where is the stock? Who can take over? And how much is still missing? This is the problem Supply Link is built to solve.

Footage: catalog, hold.

## 0:25–0:45 — SupplyLink by OceanPride

Introducing Supply Link by Ocean Pride: a smart delivery recovery solution. It connects supplier headquarters, branches, drivers, and retailers in one coordinated workflow. From the first order to the final receipt, every team can see the information needed to move fulfilment forward.

Footage: overview, branches.

## 0:45–1:20 — Choose your longyi. Know your total.

The journey starts in the retailer portal. Browse the longyi catalog, compare normal and premium styles, and choose quantities for the shop. The cart brings every selected style together. Before ordering, review the receiver, delivery address, shipping charge, and complete total. Then transfer the full amount using the supplier's payment instructions and upload payment evidence. Headquarters verifies the actual payment before confirming the order and reserving stock.

Footage: catalog, cart, checkout, payment, verified.

## 1:20–1:50 — From assignment to physical pickup.

Headquarters assigns an approved driver and prepares the delivery. Physical pickup is recorded separately from dispatch. During delivery, the driver can share location while the delivery page stays open. Headquarters and the ordering retailer can follow the road route, estimated driving time, and distance. The map supports visibility, while handover and receipt remain explicit confirmations. A location pin alone never marks the goods as received.

Footage: dispatch, driver, tracking.

## 1:50–2:40 — A branch becomes the backup.

Now imagine the original delivery cannot continue. Headquarters places the order on hold and records the issue. Before arranging a replacement, the original consignment must be resolved. Supply Link then helps headquarters coordinate a suitable branch using its dedicated wholesale backup reserve. Retail stock stays separate. In our demo, the retailer ordered sixty longyi pieces, but the branch can supply forty. Headquarters requests that first phase. Branch staff accept, reserve the selected stock, and arrange their driver. The retailer keeps the same order reference and agreed prices. Recovery becomes a visible sequence of responsibilities, quantities, and confirmations.

Footage: hold, recovery, branch_accept, branch_stock.

## 2:40–3:15 — 40 received. 20 still to deliver.

After the first delivery, Supply Link shows forty of sixty pieces received, with twenty remaining. Headquarters can arrange the outstanding twenty from its own stock or another suitable branch. Each phase keeps its own delivery progress. Every delivery requires driver handover and retailer receipt confirmation. Only after all pieces are accounted for, with no unresolved issue, does the order become Done. The completed order records one wholesale sale, even when fulfilment takes multiple deliveries.

Footage: remaining, phase_two, received, done.

## 3:15–3:40 — Keep backup stock ready.

Stock Planning helps headquarters prepare for the next disruption. Review backup availability, set targets, and respond to low-stock alerts. Where sufficient emergency-order and refill history exists, evidence-based recommendations support target decisions. Optional AI explanations clarify the saved figures. Headquarters stays in control, and incoming stock becomes available only after physical receipt is confirmed.

Footage: planning, plan_drawer, refill.

## 3:40–4:00 — A Smart Delivery Recovery Solution

For retailers, clearer progress. For branches, a defined backup role. For drivers, focused delivery work. For headquarters, coordinated recovery and accountable stock. We invite supplier partners to pilot the workflow and measure the results. Supply Link by Ocean Pride. Every order connected. Every piece accounted for.

Footage: reports, done.
